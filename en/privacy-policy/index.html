The site is not aimed at people under 18. If a minor contacts us, their data will be handled in their best interest and with authorisation from their legal guardian (article 7, Law 1581 of 2012).

Write to arelly3276@gmail.com or WhatsApp +57 310 379 0554 with your name, ID number, request and a way to reply. Queries are answered within 10 business days (extendable by 5); claims within 15 business days (extendable by 8).
