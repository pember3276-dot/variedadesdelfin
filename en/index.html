    Frequently asked questions

    Where is Variedades Delfín?

    At Calle 63 #61-23, BellaVista, Bello (Antioquia, Colombia), next to Medellín. Open every day 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m.

    How do I order?

    Add products to “My order”, enter your name and delivery option, and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. We confirm stock and final price on WhatsApp at +57 310 379 0554.

    Do you deliver in Bello and Medellín?

    Yes. Pick up free in store, use our delivery (price depends on area and time) or book DiDi Moto or another app and pay its fare.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Cash, Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia transfer and an online payment link (debit or credit card, PSE or Nequi).

    What are flash deals?

    Seasonal products in electronics, home, tools and toys at 20% off, while stock lasts.

    What else does the store sell?

    Gifts, stationery, sweets, plush toys and seasonal items for Halloween, Love and Friendship Day, Little Candles Day, the Christmas novena and Christmas.
